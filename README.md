# GeoFS-freecam

1. You need to be on a PC.

2. You need to paste the code in the console
   by clicking F12 and going to the console     tab and enter the script.

3. Change your camera view to "Free Cam"

4. Click & drag on the sky to rotate your camera view

Tip: Click shift to go faster

Tip: Use your scroll wheel to zoom in & out
